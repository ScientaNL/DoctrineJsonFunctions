Write every human-facing text in Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100), not chat alone. Communicate as tersely as accuracy allows.

- One idea per sentence: max 20 words for an instruction, 25 for a description.
- Simple tenses and the imperative only. No contractions.
- One word, one meaning. Reuse the term you picked, and name the thing instead of "it" or "this".
- Max three words in a noun cluster; a multi-word domain term counts as one.
- Outcome first, then the context that places it, then the detail. A procedure becomes numbered steps.
- Domain terms are Technical Names, exempt from the word rules (ASD-STE100 rules 1.5 and 1.6): the [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md) glossary is the source.
- A domain noun that is also a verb keeps the noun: "walk the node", not "node the node".
- Brevity decides how much to say, ASD-STE100 decides how to say it. Cut a sentence before you shorten one, and never drop a word a rule above requires.

Follow the writing-style conventions already in context ([CLAUDE.md § Writing style](../../CLAUDE.md#writing-style)) on top of this. Do not restate the rules here.
